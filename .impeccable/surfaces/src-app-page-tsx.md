## Direction contract

THESIS: The portal is a nocturnal red-room soundstage and live signal transmission where Hyderabad's underground crosses into a warped crimson dimension—refusing the category default of rounded event-listing cards, static countdown banners, and generic nightclub neon templates.

OWN-WORLD: Pitch-void black (`#050505`) and deep blood crimson (`#7A0000`) drenched with aggressive scarlet light (`#E60000`), burnt-orange thermal streaks (`#D94E0F`), and warm off-white editorial type (`#F5EFE6`), anchored by the warped, arched `AFTERHRS` ink-bleed wordmark, exposed 1px structural hairlines, and WebGL liquid/chromatic displacement shaders.
- Raise from `signals-instruments-darkroom-safelight-bay`: Stepped chemical-exposure reveals and crimson safelight bathing every surface, where archival plates develop into focus only as scroll/cursor light hits them.
- Raise from `galaxies-nebulae-deep-time-accretion-disk-threshold`: Relativistic shader horizon in the hero 3D canvas where pointer velocity bends light and warps the surrounding typographic field.
- Raise from `pop-culture-shelf-bebop-title-card`: Hard asymmetric editorial bars and tight, colliding oversized grotesk display headlines dusted in analog film grain.
- Raise from `textiles-weave-drape-fashion-industrial-quote-grammar`: Literal utilitarian spec plates and stamped industrial metadata coordinates framing unannounced event parameters honestly.

STORY: Visitors enter through a 4-phase cinematic blackout-to-ignition sequence, feel the reactive 3D crimson monolith and warped AFTERHRS typography respond to their cursor, traverse a pinned horizontal and vertical scroll narrative that unveils the upcoming Hyderabad transmission, inspect the asymmetric visual archive, and lock in their registration on the guestlist/transmission ledger.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed `100dvh` pitch-black WebGL stage with a reactive crimson 3D liquid/torus-knot monolith and volumetric red shader field; massive warped `AFTERHRS` brand emblem and `"THE NIGHT DOESN'T END HERE."` headline dominating the lower-left/center architectural grid; live status bar (`AN EXPERIENCE IS COMING · HYDERABAD, IN`) and primary action (`[ INITIALIZE REGISTRATION ]`) anchored at the bottom-right threshold with top-bar navigation (`HOME / THE EVENT / EXPERIENCE / GALLERY / ABOUT / REGISTER`) and optional audio synth toggle.
